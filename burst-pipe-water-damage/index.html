Burst pipe water damage starts the moment a pressurized line splits, and it keeps coming until the water is shut off. A supply pipe under normal pressure can move a lot of water in an hour, soaking drywall, insulation and the ceiling below.

If this is happening now, shut the main water valve first, then call the 24/7 line. While you wait, switch off power only to wet areas you can reach safely and move belongings away from the spray.

Burst pipe: what to do first

  1. Find your main shut-off valve and turn it fully closed. Most are where the water line enters the house, often in a basement or utility room.
  2. Open one faucet on the lowest level to drain what is left in the lines.
  3. Keep people and pets out of any room where water is near outlets or light fixtures.
  4. Photograph the broken pipe and the wet areas before anything is moved.
  5. Call dispatch. Tell us which floor the water is on and whether it is coming through a ceiling.

If you cannot find the valve, or it will not turn, tell dispatch when you call. Do not pry at a stuck valve, since a broken valve can make the flood worse.

Why pressurized water is different

A burst supply line is not a slow leak. The pipe holds water under pressure, so a split sprays and runs until the valve is closed. That makes this the fastest way for a few hundred gallons to reach framing, insulation and the rooms below.

Because most of the water is clean at the start, a quick response often saves flooring and cabinets that a slower response would lose. Clean supply water that sits for a day or more can still turn into a problem, since it picks up dirt as it crosses floors and soaks insulation.

Where pipes fail in West Fargo homes

The usual causes are freezing and thawing, a failed fitting, a worn connector and a loose joint. Exposed pipes in garages, crawl spaces and outside walls are the ones to watch when subzero days pile up. When a freeze is the cause, the repair steps move to the frozen pipe page.

West Fargo added many homes in the past two decades, and many of those homes are fairly new. In newer homes the failure point is more often a connector, a fitting or an appliance supply line than the pipe itself, so look at the nearest joint first.

What crews do after the pipe is repaired

A plumber fixes the pipe. A restoration crew deals with everything the water touched. The crew extracts standing water, opens wet drywall or insulation where moisture is trapped, and sets air movers and dehumidifiers to dry the rest.

What drives the cost of burst pipe cleanup

  • Water category, usually clean at the start and worse the longer it sits.
  • How many floors and rooms the water reached.
  • Materials involved, such as drywall, insulation, flooring and cabinets.
  • Drying days and the number of air movers and dehumidifiers.
  • Rebuild scope once the structure is dry.

Sudden and accidental pipe bursts are often covered by a homeowners policy, while slow leaks and neglect often are not. Protect the property from more loss, notify the insurer quickly and take reasonable steps to stop further damage, since the Insurance Department expects policyholders to do all three. Keep receipts.
