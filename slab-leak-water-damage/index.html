Slab leak water damage is damage from a water line that is leaking beneath or inside a concrete slab floor. The leak is hidden, so the first clue is often a wet spot, a warm patch on the floor or a water bill that has climbed. If water is coming up through a floor now, shut the main valve and call the 24/7 line.

Hot floor, running sounds, a high bill?

Most people do not search for the trade name. They notice a symptom. A warm spot on a floor, the sound of running water when every fixture is off, damp carpet along one wall or a meter that keeps turning are all signs of a possible line leak under a slab.

A quick check is to turn off every fixture and look at the water meter. If the dial or low-flow indicator keeps moving, water is going somewhere, and a leak detection professional can find where.

Does my home even have a slab?

That depends on the house, not the town. Slab-on-grade foundations, garage floors, additions and some commercial buildings have lines under concrete, while homes with basements typically run pipes overhead in the floor joists.

If your home has a basement, your water damage is more likely to be covered by the basement page or burst pipe page. If you have a slab garage or an addition, this page applies.

What to do first

  1. Turn off the main water valve and open a faucet to relieve pressure.
  2. Switch off power in the wet area if you can reach the panel dry.
  3. Lift rugs and furniture off the wet floor and photograph it.
  4. Call dispatch and say where the dampness is.
  5. Call a plumber or leak detection specialist to find and repair the line.

A plumber chooses the repair. The restoration crew handles the water that has already reached carpet, baseboards and drywall.

How crews dry a slab loss

Concrete holds moisture for a long time, and a slab that has soaked up water can release vapor into the room for weeks. Crews use a moisture meter on the slab, the carpet and the base of the walls, then set dehumidifiers to pull moisture out of the air and out of the floor.

Carpet and pad on a wet slab are usually removed, since they trap water against the concrete. Hard flooring is checked with meters before it is sealed again.
