Ceiling water damage is a wet, stained or sagging ceiling, and the cause can be above it. It might be a roof leak, a bathroom upstairs, a supply line or an air handler in the attic.

A ceiling holding water can drop suddenly, so treat it as an emergency, keep clear of the area, and call the 24/7 line. If a pipe is the source, shut off the main valve first, and keep power off to light fixtures in the wet area.

Sagging ceiling: what to do right now

  • Move people, pets and furniture out from under the bulge.
  • Put a bucket under any drip and do not poke the bulge unless told to, since it can release a lot of water at once.
  • Switch off power to fixtures in the area only if you can reach the panel safely.
  • Photograph stains and the room before any cleanup.
  • Call dispatch and say what is above the ceiling, such as a roof, a bathroom or an attic.

A small hole drilled by a professional can relieve trapped water in a bulging ceiling in a controlled way. Do not try it yourself.

Finding the real source of a ceiling leak

The stain you see is rarely where the leak starts. Water travels along joists and pipes before it shows up as a brown ring. A moisture meter and thermal camera trace the wet path back to the source, whether that is a roof penetration, a failed seal, a pipe, or an overflowing second-floor tub.

Roof leak damage inside the house

Roof leaks have no page of their own here, because they end up as a ceiling problem. Snow and ice are the main drivers in this part of North Dakota. Fargo's 1991 to 2020 normals show about 51 inches of snow a year, and melt that backs up behind ice at the eaves can push water under shingles and into the attic.

Wet attic insulation holds water against the ceiling drywall for a long time. A roofer stops the leak, and the restoration crew handles the wet insulation, drywall and framing below.

How a ceiling is dried and repaired

Small stains on dry drywall can be sealed and repainted. A ceiling that has sagged, softened or stayed wet for days usually needs the wet section cut out. The crew then dries the cavity with air movers and dehumidifiers and checks readings before closing it.

Hidden moisture above the ceiling is the reason this step matters. A fresh coat of paint over wet drywall hides the problem while mold risk grows. Compare with wall and drywall water damage repair where similar drying rules apply.
