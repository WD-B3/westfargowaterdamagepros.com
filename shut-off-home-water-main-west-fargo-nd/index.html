How to Shut Off Your Home Water Main in West Fargo, ND

When a pipe bursts, the first thirty seconds matter. Knowing where your main shutoff is and how it turns is the best insurance there is.

The main water shutoff is usually where the water line enters your home, often in the basement near the front wall or in a utility room. Turn the valve to the closed position, open a faucet low in the house to drain the lines and call a plumber or restoration company. Do this before the next storm, not during a leak.

Where to find it

  • In a basement, look on the wall facing the street, near the water meter.
  • In a slab home, check a utility closet, the garage or near the water heater.
  • Look for a round wheel handle or a lever handle on a pipe coming through the wall or floor.
  • Check near the meter, since the utility's valve and yours are often close together.
  • Outdoors, there may be a curb stop in a buried box, which usually needs a special key and belongs to the utility.

Your inside valve is yours to operate. The valve at the curb or in the street belongs to the city, and homeowners should not force it.

Types of valves and how they turn

Valve typeHow to close itTip
Gate valve with a round wheelTurn clockwise many times until it stopsOlder gate valves can stick or break if forced
Ball valve with a lever handleTurn a quarter turn so the handle is crosswise to the pipeEasy to operate, usually newer
Curb stopSpecial key, usually utility onlyCall the city if the inside valve fails
Fixture shutoffTurn clockwise by handValves under sinks and behind toilets handle one fixture only

Right-tight, left-loose is the rule for most wheel valves. A valve that has not moved in years may be stiff. Try it once now, gently, so you know it works. If it will not budge or leaks around the stem, call a plumber to replace it.

After the water is off

  1. Open the faucet at the lowest point in the house and the highest to drain the lines.
  2. Turn off the water heater power or gas if the heater is empty, which prevents damage.
  3. Photograph the damage and note the time.
  4. Pick up water or move items to safety.
  5. Call a plumber for the repair and a restoration company for drying.

If the pipe burst in the wall or ceiling, expect hidden moisture. Burst pipe water damage explains how crews find and dry wet cavities, and water extraction covers the first step.

When to shut it off even if nothing is wrong

  • When you leave for more than a few days, especially in winter.
  • Before replacing a toilet, faucet or other fixture.
  • When you hear running water and cannot find the source.
  • During a cold snap if a pipe has a history of freezing.

A tip from West Fargo's own utility

The City of West Fargo's Utilities page suggests turning your water valve off when you leave your home for an extended period. You still pay the base utility charge, yet a closed valve means a burst line cannot run unseen while you are gone.

The city also runs a Smart Water Meter System and Water Tracker, which can send usage alerts. Pair the two habits, since an alert is useful only if someone can act on it. Tell a neighbor or relative where your main valve is, and write it on a card.

Label it and share it

Put a bright tag on the valve and take a photo of it. Show everyone who lives in the house how it works, including older kids. Add the location to the list you keep with your insurance papers.

In rental housing, ask the landlord for the shutoff location when you move in, and store the answer on your phone. In a commercial building, the main may be shared, so ask the property manager.

Frozen and burst lines in winter

Winter breaks are the most common reason to need the valve in a hurry. What to do next is covered in frozen pipe water damage and water heater leak cleanup. In a cold snap, do not use a torch to thaw a pipe, and call a plumber instead.

Practice once a year

Pick a day each fall to walk through the shutoff routine. Locate the main, turn it off and on, check for leaks around the stem and make sure the handle moves freely. A valve that sticks is a repair you want to make on a calm day.

Also check the shutoff valves under sinks and behind toilets, and replace any that are corroded. A small fixture valve that works can keep a single leak from becoming a flood.
