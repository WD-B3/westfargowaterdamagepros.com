Water heater leak cleanup is the extraction, drying and repair work after a tank, valve or fitting releases water into the surrounding area. A typical residential tank holds roughly 40 to 50 gallons, and a failed seam can send most of it onto the floor within minutes.

If your heater is leaking now, treat it as an emergency, shut off the cold water inlet, turn off the power or gas, and call the 24/7 line.

Water heater leak: what to do first

  1. Turn off power at the breaker for an electric heater, or set a gas unit to its off position.
  2. Close the cold water inlet valve on the pipe feeding the top of the tank.
  3. Keep your hands away from the water if it is near the electrical connection or the panel.
  4. Avoid the discharge pipe and any hot water, which can scald.
  5. Photograph the tank, the label and the wet floor, then call dispatch.

Do not relight a gas heater or restore power until a plumber has checked it. Water that reaches a gas control or a wiring junction is a safety issue, not just a cleanup job.

A plumber diagnoses and replaces the heater. The restoration crew handles the water the leak released. Both jobs may be needed, and the order is usually plumber first only if the source is still running.

What the water does around a heater

Heaters sit in basements, utility rooms, closets and garages, so the water often hits concrete, a floor drain, the bottom of drywall and stored boxes first. Water that wicks into drywall behind a heater is easy to miss, and insulation in a closet wall holds moisture against the framing.

Because the water is usually clean when it leaves the tank, fast extraction and drying can save flooring and trim. Water left for days can pick up dirt and rust, and the discolored water stains concrete and carpet.

How crews clean up after a tank failure

  • Extract water with a wet vacuum or extraction unit, working toward the floor drain.
  • Check walls, trim and the floor around the heater with a moisture meter.
  • Remove wet insulation and swollen baseboard where needed.
  • Set air movers and a dehumidifier to dry the area and the rooms next to it.
  • Document readings and photos for the insurance claim.

For a larger flood in the same room, see water extraction. When the water has spread into a finished room, structural drying covers how readings guide the equipment.

What drives the cost of water heater leak cleanup

  • The amount of water released and how long it ran.
  • Flooring and wall materials it reached.
  • Whether finished space or stored belongings are involved.
  • Drying days and equipment.
  • Repairs to drywall, trim and flooring after drying.

A sudden tank failure is often covered by a homeowners policy for the resulting water damage, while the water heater itself and damage from long-term seepage often are not. Check your policy or ask your insurer. Want your real number? Call (701) 495-9075.

Catching a slow leak early

The City of West Fargo runs a Smart Water Meter System with a Water Tracker for usage alerts. A leak at a heater that runs constantly can show up there before the floor is wet. Look under the unit on a regular schedule, and see AC leaks for another common slow source in utility spaces.
