About West Fargo Water Damage Pros

A local dispatch line for water emergencies in West Fargo and nearby towns, answered around the clock.

Call (701) 495-9075

West Fargo Water Damage Pros helps homeowners and businesses in West Fargo, Fargo, Casselton and nearby communities deal with water damage. When you call, you reach a person who asks what happened and where the water is, then sends a crew with the right equipment. The office is at 3330 Sheyenne St in West Fargo.

What we do

  • Remove standing water with pumps and extraction units.
  • Map moisture in walls, floors and ceilings.
  • Dry structures with air movers and humidity control.
  • Deodorize affected areas.
  • Document the loss with photos and readings for your insurer.

Services run from water extraction to sewage backup cleanup and commercial restoration. The full list is on the services page.

How we work

We explain scope and price before work starts, and we keep records you can hand to your insurer. We do not promise what your policy will pay, since only your insurer can decide that. We are licensed and insured, and we encourage you to ask any company, including us, for credentials before you hire.

Why local matters

The flat Red River Valley floor, spring thaw, summer storms and long winters all put water into homes here. Crews who work here every season know how cold slows drying, how finished basements hold moisture and how quickly a frozen pipe can become a flood.

Dispatch serves Fargo and Casselton as well as West Fargo, and answers every day of the year, including holidays and nights.

What to expect when you call

  1. A person answers and asks where the water is and what caused it.
  2. Dispatch gives you first steps, such as shutting off the main valve.
  3. A crew is sent with pumps, extractors and drying equipment.
  4. The crew maps moisture and explains the scope before work starts.
  5. You get photos and readings to share with your insurer.

None of this requires a form or an appointment. If it is an emergency, call. If you are just planning ahead, the services page and the guides in the blog explain each type of loss.
