Why the area matters for water damage

The region is flat, with clay-heavy soil and cold winters. Frozen pipes, thaw seepage, sump failures and storm water are the usual problems, and each of them is covered on a service page. Start with frozen pipe water damage, basement water damage cleanup or storm water damage cleanup.

Businesses can read about commercial water damage restoration, and everyone can see the full list on the services page.

How dispatch plans a trip

Tell dispatch the street or road name, the nearest cross street and any gate or access notes. In winter, mention if the driveway is plowed. Rural addresses outside town may need a few more details, such as the section road or the color of the house.

Crews carry pumps, extractors and basic drying gear. Larger equipment, such as desiccant dehumidifiers, is brought when the job calls for it.
